We propose a standard resource-constrained device-focused load-sharing
simulation framework that is modular, extendable and integrates with existing
general-purpose simulation tools. To demonstrate the effectiveness of our
method, we implement the framework in OMNeT++ and investigate the performance
of several traditional load-sharing algorithms. We also propose a novel
heuristics-based space-efficient load-sharing algorithm and find that this
approach improves the fairness in task distribution across the network.
