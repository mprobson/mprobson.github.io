Reproducibility is one of the pillars of scientific research for verifiability,
benchmarking, trust, and transparency. We compared artificially generated data
samples to answer the critical question - can we reproduce the results of
generative AI models? This work provides a practical, solid foundation for AI
verification, reproducibility, and consensus for generative AI applications.
